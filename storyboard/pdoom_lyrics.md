# What the P(doom) lyrics refer to

The song is a joke about AI risk, packed with AI-safety memes. "I" is a human singing to "you", the AI; "P(doom)" is
the probability someone gives that AI ends badly for humanity, and the human keeps "upping" theirs. The tone is funny
with a little dread, not horror. Three pleading lines (ChatGPT, Sydney, Gato) are the soft breathers. Use this for
acting ideas; the storyboard decides what is shown. The songwriter's own line-by-line reading:
[osmarks' interpretation](https://docs.osmarks.net/hypha/p(doom)_song_objectively_correct_interpretation).
